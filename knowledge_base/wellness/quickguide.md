### Human as Originally Designed vs. Human in the 21st Century

| Originally Designed | 21st Century Reality |

### The Problem

The world we are living in is different to the one we are actually supposed to be living in:
- We eat products instead of food
- We live under the pressure of never-ending task lists and deadlines causing never-ending stress
- We sit instead of moving, although movement is naturally required
- We live in a man-made world full of poisons and chemicals, detached from nature

**Result:** We find ourselves living in a defected world occupied with physical, mental and environmental issues, unable to achieve our optimum life quality.

### The Four Mismatch Areas

1. **Food** - Food has lost its purpose and became entertainment and a source of toxicity
2. **Mind** - 600+ triggers per day vs. limited triggers our bodies evolved to handle
3. **Movement** - Sedentary lifestyle (sitting, escalators, elevators, TV, driving)
4. **Environment** - Polluted and toxic nature, buildings instead of green spaces

### Consequences of Mismatch

The mismatch causes three main outcomes:

#### 1. Deficiency
The body needs specific nutritional input to operate and function. When nutrition needs are not met:
- Body can't produce needed energy
- Tissues can't form naturally
- Body becomes weak and vulnerable to chronic diseases

Despite eating enough food, we don't get sufficient nutrients due to commercialization, lengthening shelf times, production techniques, and cooking methods.

#### 2. Toxaemia
Two main reasons toxins gather in the body:
- Body's exposure to unnatural environmental toxins in food, air, and water
- Inability to excrete toxins due to unhealthy habits, tiredness, and weakened nervous system

Illnesses occur because extra toxicity counterworks the body's natural immune system.

#### 3. Chronic Stress
The autonomic nervous system has two modes:
- **Sympathetic (Flight & Fight):** Responds to danger, pumps blood fueled by cortisol and glucose to arms and legs
- **Parasympathetic (Rest & Digest):** Heartbeats slow, vessels widen, intestinal movements faster; associated with rest, relaxation, and rejuvenation

**Problem:** Modern life causes chronic stress, keeping us in 24/7 flight & fight mode, putting our immune system at risk and inviting chronic diseases.

> Chronic diseases represent 93% of all deaths worldwide. Only 7% are from natural causes.

### Core Principle

> "Eat FOOD. Not too much. Mostly PLANTS." — Michael Pollan

We recommend keeping mainly on a raw and vegan diet. Cooked food, animal products, and processed food are considered indulgence and should be limited to max 20-30% of your diet.

## The Mind & Mindfulness

### Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT) Skills

Four critical skills for emotional balance:
1. **Mindfulness** - Being fully present with direct experience of the moment
2. **Emotion Regulation** - Managing emotional responses
3. **Distress Tolerance** - Coping with difficult situations
4. **Interpersonal Effectiveness** - Communicating and relating to others

### Mindfulness Defined

Being mindful is to be fully present with your direct experience of the moment. Fully present with whatever you are doing, thinking, or feeling. **HERE AND NOW.**

### Mindful Attitudes

- **Non-Judging:** Work on not constantly generating judgments about experiences
- **Patience:** Accept that things must unfold in their own time
- **Beginner's Mind:** Experience everything as if for the first time
- **Trust:** Develop basic trust in yourself and your feelings
- **Acceptance:** See things as they actually are in the present
- **Letting Go:** Cultivate non-attachment

### Autopilot Mode

**The Problem:** When we slip into autopilot mode, our attention is absorbed in our wandering minds and we are not really 'present' in our own lives.

**Consequences:**
- Fail to notice the beauty of life
- Fail to hear what our bodies are telling us
- Get stuck in mechanical, conditioned ways of thinking
- Become vulnerable to anxiety, stress, depression, and reactivity

> Research shows that the more our minds ponder, the less happy we become.

### Flight or Fight Mode

Flight or fight responses are innately programmed for survival. The body perceives any trigger (from being chased by a lion to being late for a meeting) as life-threatening danger.

**The Problem:** Today we face numerous unjustified triggers, keeping us constantly in sympathetic mode, preventing rejuvenation and healing.

**Rejuvenating Mode:** The body is totally relaxed and focused on repairing and recreating itself. Humans should have only a few moments of stress during a regular day with rejuvenation in between.

### Brain Waves & State of Mind

| Wave | Frequency | State |
|------|-----------|-------|
| **Gamma** | 40-200 Hz | Connected to higher consciousness |
| **Beta** | 13-40 Hz | Alert, attentive, focused on mental activity, problem solving |
| **Alpha** | 7-13 Hz | "Power of now," resting state, calmness, alertness, learning |
| **Theta** | 4-7 Hz | Deep meditation, gateway to learning, memory, intuition |
| **Delta** | 0-4 Hz | Deepest meditation, dreamless sleep, healing and regeneration |

**Goal:** Be mostly in low-frequency waves (Alpha and Theta) for a healthier state of mind.

### Reference Frame

Our reference frame is built from:
1. Genetic inheritance from parents
2. Family environment
3. Culture and religion
4. Education and schooling
5. Work environment
6. Social life and experiences

This frame determines how we perceive the world and react to triggers.

### Action vs. Reaction

**Reaction:** Sporadic, emotional, autopilot-based
**Action:** Balanced with rational thinking, conscious decision-making

> There is a space between the trigger and response. In that space lies our power to choose our response.

### The Habit Loop

**Components:**
- **Cue:** The trigger that stimulates the habitual act
- **Routine:** The action you take (the habit itself)
- **Reward:** The benefit you get from doing the habit

### K.I.S.S. Method (Keep, Improve, Start, Stop)

Categorize habits by urgency and importance:

| Habit | Strategy |
|-------|----------|
| High urgency, high importance | **Stop** immediately |
| Low urgency, high importance | **Improve** over time |
| High urgency, low importance | **Keep** monitoring |
| Low urgency, low importance | **Start** addressing |

## Movement

### The Problem

Our body is designed for a physical and active life. However, today people:
- Sit at desks for hours
- Use only fingers to type
- Watch TV and play games
- Use escalators and elevators
- Drive everywhere

### Benefits of Movement

- Circulation of lymphatic system for detox and rejuvenation
- Body can rest, heal, and renew after activity
- Prevention of toxin accumulation
- Protection against chronic diseases

### Two Effective Approaches

1. **Weekly Practice:** Find a yoga or pilates class you enjoy and commit to once a week. One hour of yoga, pilates, or yin yoga can make a big difference.

2. **Interval Training:** 
   - 30 seconds intensive pace
   - 90 seconds slow pace (recovery)
   - 2-minute cycles × 8 repetitions = 16 minutes
   - Follow with stretching

> A 20-30 minute routine of interval exercises and bodyweight exercises is a quick and effective workout solution.

### Surya Namaskar (Sun Salutation)

A set of 12 asanas done one after another. Best practiced at sunrise or sunset. Provides perfect flexibility to the body.

## Mindful Practices

### Mindful Eating

1. Observe the food on your plate
2. Use a reminder for mindful eating (special fork, etc.)
3. Smell, taste, and bite slowly
4. Put utensils down after each bite
5. Chew well and enjoy flavors
6. Be silent while chewing
7. Feel food moving downward after swallowing
8. Repeat for each meal

### Mindful Shopping

1. Set your budget
2. Make a list of items you REALLY need
3. Set criteria: Better for health? Better for planet and society?
4. Be present while shopping
5. Read labels on every product
6. Breathe slowly to slow thoughts
7. Make sure purchases feel right inside

### Meditation

1. Create time and space; choose a regular time
2. Start with 5 minutes, increase to 15-40 minutes
3. Find comfortable sitting position
4. Check posture: sit up straight
5. Take deep breaths
6. Direct attention to breathing
7. Keep attention on breathing
8. Be kind to yourself if focus drifts
9. When timer goes off, keep eyes closed until ready

### Mindful Walking

1. **Preparing:** Focus on rhythmic breathing
2. **Walking:** Focus on steps at slow, even, relaxed pace
3. **Noticing:** Become aware of feet in contact with ground
4. **Seeing:** Focus on surroundings, colors, light, shadows
5. **Hearing:** Focus on sounds without judging
6. **Smelling:** Slow down to savor scents
7. **Touching:** Feel textures (petals, leaves)
8. **Feeling:** Notice moods without judgment; let them pass
9. **Finishing:** Focus attention on present moment before continuing day

### Turmeric Latte
- 350ml almond milk
- 1 tbsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp cinnamon powder
- 1 tsp cardamom powder
- 1 tsp ginger powder
- ¼ tsp chili powder
- 2 drops vanilla extract
- Mix with a shaker

### Almond Milk
- 1 glass raw almonds (soaked 7-8 hours)
- 3 glasses alkaline water
- Blend with high-speed blender
- Filter with thin cloth (optional)
- Store in glass container, refrigerate, use within 3 days

### Bulletproof Coffee
- ½ spoon organic coffee
- ½ spoon cacao butter or coconut oil
- ½ tsp cacao powder (optional)
- 200ml boiling water
- Blend or stir

### Chia Pudding
- 250ml almond milk
- 60-100g blackberries (or fruit of choice)
- 1 tbsp agave syrup
- 3 tbsp chia seeds
- Blend all except chia seeds
- Add chia seeds, mix briefly (or stir with fork)

### Avocado Banana Chocolate Pudding

- 6 dates
- 1 banana
- 3 tbsp cocoa powder
- 2 drops vanilla extract
- 1 cup coconut or almond milk
- Blend until pudding texture
